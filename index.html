Short tests designed around measurable performance.

01

Reaction Speed

Measure how quickly you respond to a visual signal.

Best: —
Wait through an unpredictable delay until the panel turns green, then click immediately. Clicking before the signal is a false start and never becomes a reaction-time score.
02

Gridshot

Measure flick accuracy, target acquisition and hand-eye coordination.

Best: —
Hit as many targets as possible in 20 seconds. Gridshot measures target count and flick accuracy. A small crosshair keeps the visual reference precise without covering the target.
03

Visual Memory

Remember increasingly difficult spatial patterns.

Best: —
Memorize a group of highlighted locations and reproduce them after they disappear. Each successful round increases the number of locations.
04

Number Memory

Test how many digits you can hold accurately.

Best: —
Memorize a number, enter it after it disappears, and progress to longer sequences. One incorrect sequence ends the run.
05

Typing Speed

Measure WPM and typing accuracy with a timed paragraph.

Best: —
Type the displayed paragraph during the fixed test. The passage continues automatically so fast typists cannot run out of text.
